A few helpful answers

Questions you may have.

What is Friendly Conversation?

A chance to talk with a WellNow conversation provider by chat or call. It is not a substitute for professional counselling, diagnosis or emergency support.

How do I book a session?

Choose a service, send a booking request and wait for WellNow to confirm the provider, time and fee.

Can I choose a counsellor?

You can explore profiles that are available. WellNow will confirm availability and help coordinate a suitable professional.

Do I pay on this website?

Online checkout is not active in this draft. WellNow must confirm the fee and provide valid payment instructions before you pay.

Is WellNow an emergency service?

No. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Can providers join WellNow?

Yes, interested providers can send an enquiry. Professional qualifications and applicable registrations must be reviewed before a counsellor profile is published.
